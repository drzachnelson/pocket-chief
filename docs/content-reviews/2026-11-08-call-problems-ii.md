## Needs sign-off — per entry

- [ ] **Acute Mesenteric Ischemia:** complete clinical page, anticoagulant regimens and observational outcome ranges. Preserve SCORE’s NOMI heparin exclusion versus [WSES 2022](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13017-022-00443-x) correction of low flow with optional systemic heparin and preoperative anticoagulation. [ESVS 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejvs.2025.06.010) MVT/poststent durations differ from WSES. Dose/ACT examples are institutional or VTE extrapolations, not universal AMI standards. CT and adjunctive viability performance, cause frequencies and series denominators remain explicitly bounded; a normal lactate or benign early bowel appearance cannot exclude ischemia.
- [ ] **Necrotizing Soft-Tissue Infections:** complete clinical page, drug choices/doses, timing and outcome ranges. M protein is antiphagocytic rather than the GAS superantigen; low LRINEC does not exclude NSTI. WSES, EAST, IDSA and SCORE use differing initial/relook clocks, all preserved. [IDSA](https://www.idsociety.org/practice-guideline/skin-and-soft-tissue-infections/) duration is tied to source control, improvement and 48–72 hours afebrile. [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/vibrio/hcp/clinical-overview/) permits selected pediatric doxycycline/fluoroquinolone treatment for Vibrio and describes 7–14 days, correcting blanket prohibitions and a mandatory ≥2-week claim. Fournier organ preservation/diversion is individualized. Unverified fivefold timing cliffs, potentially inadequate aztreonam allergy wording, universal aminoglycoside additions and misleading drug-label ceilings were not copied as mandates.
- [ ] **Esophageal Perforation:** complete clinical page, exposure/repair details, antifungal selection, severity scoring and device outcomes. [WSES 2019](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13017-019-0245-2) and the verified STS 2026 abstract support CT-based initial assessment in stable patients; the latter’s full text was not available. A series’ 100% CT NPV is not a universal rule-out. Delay alone does not preclude repair. Stent/EVT closure requires drainage and nutrition; comparative percentages are selection-dependent. Hamman, rather than the OCR’s “Hartmann,” is the accepted crunch eponym. Routine antifungal therapy, surveillance schedules and practice-specific technique remain review items.
- [ ] **Pilonidal Disease:** complete disease page and recurrence/evidence comparisons. Acquired hair reaction, abscess drainage and extent-based chronic care are retained. [ASCRS 2019](https://doi.org/10.1097/DCR.0000000000001237) and the [German update](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8097120/) favor avoiding midline closure. Fixed drainage distance, sitting thresholds and hygiene causation are not validated rules. The [laser trial](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamasurgery/fullarticle/2811756) reports 10.4% versus 33.6% at one year; longer-term durability is uncertain. Suspicious “13 weeks faster” marsupialization and a retrospective 219-fold combined risk were not promoted into causal estimates. Full operative steps remain the separate procedure scope.
- [ ] **Breast Abscess and Mastitis:** complete clinical page, antibiotic/lactation cautions, drainage, granulomatous disease and cancer-exclusion decisions. The guideline titled [ASBrS/SBI/CAP 2025](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamasurgery/fullarticle/2847015) was published in 2026. Distinguish sterile inflammation from bacterial disease. Preserve SCORE aspiration practice versus current drainage guidance; the 3 cm boundary requires judgment, and loculated/solidified collections can need OR drainage. [ABM #36](https://doi.org/10.1089/bfm.2022.29207.kbm) supports physiologic feeding/pumping rather than forced emptying or a universal pumping ban; milk-fistula cessation teaching remains a visible disagreement. Fiser does cover Mondor disease. Persistent findings need malignancy evaluation; the Word’s exact NCCN 2026 attribution was not directly retrieved. Drug-label ceilings, unverified prolonged-doxycycline cutoffs and a universal IBC survival percentage were not copied as general rules.
- [ ] **Gastrointestinal Foreign Bodies:** complete clinical page, object-specific adult/pediatric timing and follow-up. [Poison Center guidance](https://www.poison.org/battery/guideline) supports honey from **at least 12 months**, not strictly older than 12 months; a missing halo/step-off does not exclude a battery. Delayed battery injury and herald bleeding remain urgent. Pediatric glucagon dosing is supplied-review content requiring specific medication review; drugs must not delay endoscopy. - [ ] **Abdominal Compartment Syndrome:** complete clinical page, medical/PCD/decompression/open-abdomen sequencing and pediatric definitions.
