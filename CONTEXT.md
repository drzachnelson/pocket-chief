# Pocket Chief

A private general surgery reference for one user, the owner. This file is the glossary: one entry per term, in the words the app and its tickets use. Decisions live in `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md` and the ticket resolutions, not here. `_Not_` names what a term does not mean, or a word not to use for it.

## Content

**Topic**:
A SCORE-aligned study note, placed in the SCORE taxonomy.
_Not_: a playbook.

**Playbook**:
An operative guide organised by procedure and approach, in a corpus of its own that renders the same blocks as a topic.
_Not_: a topic. It has no place in the SCORE taxonomy.

**Library**:
The developer word for the full content set.
_Not_: UI copy. The UI says Topics and Playbooks.

**Category**:
A top-level SCORE node.
_Not_: a section, or a specialty.

**Group**:
A category's second level, such as Diseases & Conditions or Operations & Procedures. A category may hold topics directly instead.
_Not_: a specialty.

**Section**:
A heading inside a topic or playbook page, listed under "On this page" in Contents.
_Not_: a taxonomy level. Use Category or Group.

**SCORE path**:
A topic's category and group, in that order, as shown in lists and breadcrumbs.
_Not_: shown with a "SCORE · " prefix.

**Specialty**:
A playbook's service line, such as Vascular or Colorectal. It is free text on the playbook and the heading the Playbooks index lists it under.
_Not_: a category, a section, or a node in the SCORE taxonomy.

**Approach**:
How a playbook's operation is done: open, laparoscopic, robotic or endovascular. It is a label and a search term.
_Not_: a filter or a group.

## Reading state

**Saved**:
A topic or playbook the owner has bookmarked, kept on this device.
_Not_: Favorites. "Bookmark" is not UI copy.

**Studied**:
The reader-progress mark on a topic.
_Not_: Reviewed.

**Reviewed**:
Editorial approval of content, shown in a topic's History.
_Not_: reader progress. That is Studied.

**Recently viewed**:
The topics and playbooks opened on this device, newest first.
_Not_: Resume. No page is singled out to resume.

**Recently added**:
The topics and playbooks new to the app, by review date, grouped by day.
_Not_: Recently viewed.

## Pages

**Home**:
The start page: the shared search field plus Recently viewed, Saved and Recently added.
_Not_: a navigation page. It never restates the navigation, and it is not the results page.

**Index**:
The page that lists one corpus: the Topics index lists categories, the Playbooks index lists specialties.
_Not_: Home, or a category page.

**Category page**:
One category's groups and topics.
_Not_: the Topics index.

**Reader page**:
A topic page or a playbook page.
_Not_: any other page. Everything else is a browse or utility page.

**List row**:
The one hairline row that lists a page or category: title, optional quiet line, optional trailing text, chevron.
_Not_: a card or a tile. There is no playbook card.

**Disclaimer line**:
"Educational reference only. Not patient-specific guidance.", shown once at the end of every topic and playbook page.
_Not_: the sidebar privacy note or the education label.

## Navigation and page controls

**Tab bar**:
The phone's bottom navigation on non-reader pages.
_Not_: the page toolbar, which takes its place on reader pages.

**Rail**:
The labelled tablet navigation.
_Not_: the sidebar.

**Sidebar**:
The laptop navigation: pinned navigation, Contents on reader pages only, and the foot.
_Not_: the rail.

**Foot**:
The pinned bottom of the sidebar, holding Settings.
_Not_: a place for the Disclaimer line.

**Top row**:
The sticky row above the page that holds the shared search field, and on reader pages the page toolbar, on tablet and laptop. On phone the same row is called the header row, and the page toolbar sits at the bottom instead.
_Not_: the page toolbar itself.

**Page toolbar**:
The controls for the open reader page: Back, Index, Contents, Find text and Expand all, with slots varying by width.
_Not_: the tab bar.

**Page tools**:
The page toolbar's accessible name.
_Not_: a visible label.

**Find bar**:
The field, match count, Previous, Next and Close that Find text opens. It takes the page toolbar's place in the top row (on phone, the toolbar's row) and is named Find in page.
_Not_: the shared search field, or a second search landmark.

**Contents**:
The open reader page's sections plus the other pages of its corpus.
_Not_: Browse, the button it replaces.

**Contents dialog**:
Contents shown as one native dialog: a bottom sheet on phone, a side panel on tablet.
_Not_: the laptop Contents, which is inline in the sidebar and is not a dialog.

## Finding things

**Search**:
Finds a topic or playbook anywhere in the app, by name or by words in its text. One field, one matcher.
_Not_: Find text, or a filter.

**Find text**:
Locates words inside the open reader page.
_Not_: Search.

**Name**:
A title, alias, SCORE category or tag, and for a playbook also its specialty and approach. A name match outranks a body match.
_Not_: body text.

**Quick results**:
The short ranked list under the search field.
_Not_: the results page.

**Results page**:
The page that lists every match for a search.
_Not_: Home, or quick results.

**Filter**:
Narrowing a browse list by an attribute.
_Not_: search. Approach is not a filter.
