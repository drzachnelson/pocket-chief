# Pocket Chief

Pocket Chief is a private, installable general surgery reference for Zach. It converts user-supplied study notes and images into concise, source-linked pages organized by the editable SCORE curriculum, then makes those pages rapidly searchable on phone and desktop.

Success means Zach can paste a note packet, review an AI-composed page, approve it, find it quickly online or offline, and turn a selected high-yield point into a reviewed Anki cloze card. The system must never publish an unsupported AI claim silently or accept patient information.

V1 ships the complete workflow and one supplied topic, choledocholithiasis. Four additional launch topics are populated from the next four source packets Zach provides.
